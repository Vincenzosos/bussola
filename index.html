This is a public, read-only snapshot of SV Terminal, a personal app that runs on its owner's computer. The Trading Lab trades pretend money on real Lang & Schwarz prices (indicative quotes, fetched for personal use), with Trade Republic's €1 fee and the 2026 transaction taxes; it places no orders and has no link to any broker. My Portfolio is the owner's real money and is published only encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Snapshots: about every 30 minutes 07:30–23:00 Berlin on weekdays while the owner's computer runs, and after the lab's nightly step. Charts: TradingView Lightweight Charts.
